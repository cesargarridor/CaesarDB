Pricing and comparison

Free to explore, Pro to operate. One-time payment, no subscription.

Localhost is always free: with localhost, 127.0.0.1 or ::1 (DynamoDB Local or the CaesarDB emulator) everything is unlocked, Free included.

On AWS, Free is read-only, with 1 active tab and PartiQL SELECT. Writing, migrating or administering on AWS requires Pro. The lock is enforced in the backend (Rust), not just in the interface.
