Frequently asked questions.

Is it free on localhost?

Yes. With DynamoDB Local or the CaesarDB emulator everything is unlocked, Free included.

What does Free include on AWS?

Read-only, one active tab and PartiQL SELECT. You need Pro to write to AWS.

Does it send my data or credentials anywhere?

No. Zero telemetry. It only validates the license with Polar and checks GitHub Releases for updates.

How many machines can I use my license on?

Up to 3 machines, managed from the Polar portal.

Who is responsible for writes and AWS costs?

You are. Operations run with your IAM permissions; keep backups (PITR) and watch your costs. See the Terms.

Is it open source?

No: it is closed-source, distributed as signed binaries.
