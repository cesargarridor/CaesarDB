What it is

A native client to explore, query and operate your DynamoDB tables, on AWS or on your machine, with safety first.

Everything you need

01Queries

Scan and Query by PK, SK, GSI and LSI, a visual filter builder and PartiQL with Cmd+Enter. Real metrics: RCU, scanned vs returned and latency.

02DataGrid and Inspector

Virtualized grid, Clean/Raw JSON and tree Inspector, detectors for TTL, JSON in strings and binaries, and exact item size against 400 KB.

03Single-Table Design

Group and filter by PK/SK prefix or entityType, hide empty columns and compare two items side by side.

04Read-Only and prod safety

Read-Only enforced in Rust, prod environments opened read-only, queued changes with diff and confirmation, and local undo.

05Migration

Clone the AWS schema to local, copy or move items across tables, profiles and regions, and import/export JSON, NDJSON and CSV with backoff.

06Observability

DynamoDB Streams Live Tail with OldImage/NewImage diff and CloudWatch metrics (RCU, WCU, throttling, latency).

07MCP server

Let an AI agent query your tables: read-only by default, no PartiQL and per-call caps. It acts with your IAM permissions.

08Local emulator

Launch a local DynamoDB in 1 click, no Docker. On localhost everything is unlocked, Free included.

FAQ

Is it free on localhost?

Yes. With DynamoDB Local or the CaesarDB emulator everything is unlocked, Free included.

What does Free include on AWS?

Read-only, one active tab and PartiQL SELECT. To write to AWS you need Pro.

Does it send my data or credentials anywhere?

No. Zero telemetry. It only validates the license with Polar and checks for updates on GitHub Releases.

On how many machines can I use my license?

Up to 3 machines, managed from the Polar portal.

Who is responsible for writes and AWS costs?

You are. Operations run with your IAM permissions; keep backups (PITR) and watch your costs. See the Terms.

Is it open source?

No: it is closed-source, distributed as signed binaries.
