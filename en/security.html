Security

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Summary. Local-first, credentials encrypted on your machine, zero telemetry, signed updates and a bounded MCP server. Operations run with your IAM permissions and at your responsibility.

Credentials and local data

  • Secret keys, session tokens and proxy credentials live in a local store encrypted with AES-256-GCM, with permissions restricted to the user. They never leave your machine.
  • The license key is stored encrypted and bound to the machine.
  • Error messages hide credentials in URLs and proxies.

Safeguards when operating

  • Read-Only mode is enforced in the backend (Rust), not only in the interface; connections tagged prod open read-only.
  • On AWS with the Free plan, PartiQL accepts SELECT only.
  • Confirmations and a change review (Staged Changes) before writing, plus local undo.

MCP server

Read-only by default; it does not expose PartiQL; every call has caps (one request, ≤ 100 items, 1 MB). Write tools must be enabled explicitly and do not work in Read-Only. Still, the MCP acts with your credentials and IAM permissions and at your responsibility: use a least-privilege role.

Updates and distribution

CaesarDB is closed-source and distributed as signed binaries. Updates are downloaded from GitHub Releases and signature-verified before installation. Dependencies are audited regularly.

Your part

Keep backups enabled (PITR), use least-privilege credentials, enable your operating system’s disk encryption and watch your AWS costs.

Report a vulnerability

Write to cgarrui0301@gmail.com with a description, steps to reproduce, version and operating system. We ask for responsible disclosure: do not publish the flaw before it is fixed. We appreciate the report and will reply as soon as possible.
