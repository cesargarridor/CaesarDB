Refunds and withdrawal

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Summary. Payment is handled by Polar as merchant of record. We offer a 14-day courtesy period (editable text below) and respect consumers’ non-waivable rights.

Who handles payment

CaesarDB purchases are charged and managed by Polar as merchant of record: it issues the receipt, calculates taxes and processes refunds. Polar’s own terms and refund policy, published on its site, also apply. Your receipts and activations are in the Polar customer portal.

14-day refund

14 days, no questions asked. If you ask within 14 days of purchase, we refund the full amount. Write to cgarrui0301@gmail.com with the email you paid with, or request it from your receipt or the Polar portal.

The money goes back to the same payment method, usually within 5-10 business days (depends on your bank). A refunded license is deactivated. After 14 days no refund is given, unless the law requires it. This policy is voluntary, may be changed for future purchases and does not affect your statutory rights.

Right of withdrawal (EU consumers)

EU consumers have 14 days to withdraw from a distance purchase. For digital content not supplied on a tangible medium (such as a downloadable software license), that right is lost once performance has begun with your prior express consent and your acknowledgement that you lose the right of withdrawal. When you start the download or the license activation, after accepting that condition, the right of withdrawal lapses. The 14-day courtesy period above is separate and additional while it remains in force.

Statutory guarantee

If the software does not conform to what was contracted (for example, a defect that prevents use and that we cannot fix), you are entitled to the statutory guarantee of conformity (repair, replacement, price reduction or termination) under applicable consumer law. These texts do not limit that.

How to ask

Write to cgarrui0301@gmail.com with the purchase email and the reason (optional). You can also start the process from the Polar portal.
