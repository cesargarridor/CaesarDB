Support

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Summary. Write to us with your version and operating system. For licenses and receipts use the Polar portal.

Contact

Email: cgarrui0301@gmail.com. We reply as soon as we can, usually within a few business days. Include the CaesarDB version (Settings → About), your operating system, what you were trying to do and the error message (without credentials).

Licenses and billing

Activations (up to 3 machines), receipts and a lost machine: in the Polar customer portal. For refunds see Refunds.

Before writing

  • Connection failing? Check the AWS profile, region, proxy and that the credentials have IAM permissions.
  • A feature says “requires Pro”? On AWS the Free plan is read-only; on localhost everything is free. See Pricing.
  • Security: report vulnerabilities through the channel given.

CaesarDB is closed-source software, distributed as signed binaries. No cookies, no trackers and no telemetry. This site is static and loads no external resources.
